# Specification: Platform-Wide UX Overhaul & Mobile-First Optimization

## Overview
A comprehensive UI/UX refinement track focused on three pillars: technical fixes (carousel, z-index), architectural shift (global mobile-first optimization), and asset management (comprehensive image audit and data legitimacy). The goal is to elevate the platform to the "Law of Luxury" standard with rich micro-interactions and verified accuracy.

## Functional Requirements

### 1. Hero Section Refinements
- **Logo Carousel:** Implement a seamless, infinite auto-sliding animation loop for the brand logo carousel in the hero section.
- **Tooltip Logic:** Fix z-index stacking issues ensuring tooltips are always visible on top of all other components.

### 2. Global Mobile-First Overhaul
- **Unified Strategy:** Audit and refactor every page, portal, and dashboard component to prioritize mobile layout and usability.
- **Customer Portal:** Ensure full functionality and luxury aesthetic on small screens (Fleet management, Bookings, Loyalty).
- **Admin OS:** Optimize complex data views and management tools for mobile accessibility without data loss.

### 3. Comprehensive Image Asset Audit
- **Global Inventory:** Create a master list of every image appearing across the public site, Customer Portal, and Admin OS.
- **Technical Specs:** Define exact dimensions for every placeholder to ensure high-density display compatibility.
- **Art Direction:** For each placeholder, provide a detailed description of the "Ideal Asset" that suits the luxury context (e.g., "High-contrast shot of a ceramic-coated BMW M4 in Abu Dhabi evening light").

### 4. Data & Contact Integrity
- **Standardization:** Perform a global audit to ensure every phone number, address, social handle, and link matches the verified Smart Motor identity (Musaffah M9).
- **Legitimacy Check:** Verify all external links and handles against official Google Business profiles and social accounts.

### 5. Rich Interactive Experience
- **Micro-Reactions:** Implement multi-layered feedback for all interactive elements (Tactile, Luminous, and Fluid Motion).
- **Hover States:** Add subtle scale-downs, silver-shine highlights, and elegant Framer Motion transitions.
- **Error Handling:** Ensure feedback and error states feel premium rather than clinical.

## Non-Functional Requirements
- **Performance:** Maintain high Lighthouse scores despite increased animations.
- **Responsiveness:** Zero horizontal scrolling on screens down to 320px width.
- **Luxury Aesthetic:** Every interaction must feel "At home on a Rolls-Royce dashboard."

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Hero logo carousel loops infinitely and automatically.
- [ ] No tooltip overlap issues remain across the site.
- [ ] All 100+ routes pass mobile-usability tests.
- [ ] Master Image List created with dimensions and descriptions.
- [ ] All contact info verified and standardized globally.
- [ ] Buttons and links respond with high-quality micro-animations.

## Out of Scope
- Creation/Generation of the actual final image files (only listing and planning).
- Content writing for new blog posts (only layout optimization).
